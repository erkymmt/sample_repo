# Step 2: Protocol Foundation (Sections 1-6)

## Purpose
Generate the foundational sections of the clinical trial protocol.

## Prerequisites
- `waypoints/intervention_metadata.json`
- `waypoints/01_clinical_research_summary.json`

## Sections to Generate

### Section 1: Title and Protocol Information
- Protocol title
- Protocol ID
- Version and date
- Sponsor information

### Section 2: Synopsis
- Brief study overview
- Study design summary
- Key objectives

### Section 3: Introduction and Background
- Scientific rationale
- Intervention background
- Relevant literature

### Section 4: Study Objectives
- Primary objective
- Secondary objectives
- Exploratory objectives

### Section 5: Study Design
- Study type (RCT, single-arm, etc.)
- Treatment groups
- Randomization scheme
- Blinding strategy

### Section 6: Study Population
- Inclusion criteria
- Exclusion criteria
- Target enrollment

## Output
Save to `waypoints/02_protocol_foundation.md`

## Completion
Update metadata and display: "✓ Step 2 complete - Protocol foundation generated"
