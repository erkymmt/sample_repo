## Section 7: Study Intervention

#### 7.1.2 Placebo

| Parameter | Specification |
|-----------|---------------|
| **Formulation** | Enteric-coated tablet |
| **Appearance** | Identical to active aspirin |
| **Composition** | Inert excipients only |
| **Manufacturer** | Same as active product |
| **Storage** | Same as active product |

### 7.2 Dosing and Administration

#### 7.2.1 Dose Regimen

| Treatment Arm | Dose | Route | Frequency | Duration |
|---------------|------|-------|-----------|----------|
| Aspirin | 100 mg | Oral | Once daily | 24 months |
| Placebo | 1 tablet | Oral | Once daily | 24 months |

#### 7.2.2 Administration Instructions

1. **Timing:** Take study medication at approximately the same time each day, preferably in the morning
2. **With Food:** May be taken with or without food; taking with food may reduce GI discomfort
3. **Swallow Whole:** Do not crush, chew, or split the enteric-coated tablet
4. **Missed Dose:** If a dose is missed, take it as soon as remembered unless within 12 hours of next scheduled dose; do not double dose
5. **Water:** Take with a full glass of water (240 mL)

#### 7.2.3 Dose Modifications

**No dose modifications are permitted.** Participants unable to tolerate the assigned dose should:
1. Contact the study site for evaluation
2. Be assessed for adverse events
3. Consider discontinuation if intolerance persists

### 7.3 Drug Accountability

- Study drug will be dispensed at randomization and subsequent visits
- Participants must return all unused medication and empty containers
- Drug accountability records will be maintained at each site
- Compliance will be calculated based on pill counts and diary review

### 7.4 Treatment Compliance

#### 7.4.1 Compliance Assessment Methods

| Method | Frequency |
|--------|-----------|
| Pill count | Every visit |
| Participant diary | Daily |
| Self-report questionnaire | Every visit |

#### 7.4.2 Compliance Definitions

- **Compliant:** ≥80% of expected doses taken
- **Non-compliant:** <80% of expected doses taken

#### 7.4.3 Non-Compliance Management

For non-compliant participants:
1. Reinforce importance of adherence
2. Identify and address barriers to compliance
3. Implement reminder strategies (phone calls, texts)
4. Document reasons for non-compliance

### 7.5 Concomitant Medications and Therapies

#### 7.5.1 Required Background Therapy

All participants should receive guideline-directed medical therapy (GDMT) for CCS:

| Medication Class | Recommendation |
|-----------------|----------------|
| Statins | Required (unless contraindicated) |
| ACE inhibitors/ARBs | Recommended for high-risk patients |
| Beta-blockers | Per investigator discretion |
| Antianginal therapy | As needed for symptom control |

#### 7.5.2 Prohibited Medications

The following are **prohibited** during study participation:

| Category | Examples | Rationale |
|----------|----------|-----------|
| **Other antiplatelet agents** | Clopidogrel, prasugrel, ticagrelor | Confounds efficacy assessment |
| **Oral anticoagulants** | Warfarin, rivaroxaban, apixaban, dabigatran, edoxaban | Increased bleeding risk |
| **Non-study aspirin** | Any aspirin products | Protocol violation |
| **Chronic NSAIDs** | Ibuprofen, naproxen (chronic use) | Drug interaction, CV risk |

#### 7.5.3 Permitted Medications

| Category | Notes |
|----------|-------|
| Short-term NSAIDs | Up to 7 days for acute pain (document use) |
| Proton pump inhibitors | Recommended for GI protection |
| H2 blockers | Alternative for GI protection |
| Antacids | As needed |

#### 7.5.4 Rescue Medications

- For breakthrough angina: Sublingual nitroglycerin as needed
- Document all rescue medication use in eDiary

### 7.6 Treatment Discontinuation

#### 7.6.1 Criteria for Discontinuation

Study treatment should be discontinued if:

1. **Bleeding Events:**
   - Intracranial hemorrhage
   - Life-threatening bleeding (BARC 5)
   - Major bleeding requiring transfusion (≥2 units)

2. **Allergic Reactions:**
   - Anaphylaxis or severe hypersensitivity
   - Severe aspirin-exacerbated respiratory disease

3. **Clinical Events:**
   - Initiation of required oral anticoagulation
   - Initiation of DAPT for clinical indication
   - Surgery requiring antiplatelet discontinuation >14 days

4. **Other:**
   - Pregnancy
   - Participant request
   - Investigator judgment (safety)

#### 7.6.2 Post-Discontinuation Follow-up

Participants who discontinue treatment will be:
- Followed for primary endpoint (unless consent withdrawn)
- Contacted at scheduled visit windows
- Included in intention-to-treat analysis

### 8.2 Efficacy Assessments

#### 8.2.1 Primary Endpoint Assessment

**MACE (Major Adverse Cardiovascular Events):**

| Component | Definition | Adjudication |
|-----------|------------|--------------|
| **Cardiovascular Death** | Death from MI, stroke, heart failure, sudden cardiac death, or other CV cause | CEC adjudicated |
| **Non-fatal Myocardial Infarction** | Per Fourth Universal Definition of MI (2018) | CEC adjudicated |
| **Non-fatal Stroke** | Ischemic or hemorrhagic stroke with neurological deficit >24 hours | CEC adjudicated |

**Clinical Events Committee (CEC):**
- Independent committee blinded to treatment assignment
- Reviews all potential endpoint events
- Adjudicates based on pre-specified criteria

#### 8.2.2 Secondary Efficacy Endpoints

| Endpoint | Assessment Method | Timing |
|----------|-------------------|--------|
| All-cause mortality | Vital status ascertainment | Throughout |
| Individual MACE components | CEC adjudication | Throughout |
| Coronary revascularization | Medical records review | Throughout |
| Hospitalization for unstable angina | Medical records review | Throughout |
| Seattle Angina Questionnaire (SAQ) | Patient-reported | Baseline, M6, M12, M18, M24 |
| EQ-5D-5L | Patient-reported | Baseline, M6, M12, M18, M24 |

### 8.3 Safety Assessments

#### 8.3.1 Bleeding Events

**Classification System:** Bleeding Academic Research Consortium (BARC)

| BARC Type | Definition |
|-----------|------------|
| **Type 0** | No bleeding |
| **Type 1** | Bleeding not actionable and not causing patient to seek care |
| **Type 2** | Any clinically overt bleeding that does not meet criteria for Types 3-5 |
| **Type 3a** | Overt bleeding plus Hb drop 3-<5 g/dL OR transfusion |
| **Type 3b** | Overt bleeding plus Hb drop ≥5 g/dL, tamponade, or surgical intervention |
| **Type 3c** | Intracranial hemorrhage or intraocular bleeding |
| **Type 4** | CABG-related bleeding |
| **Type 5a** | Probable fatal bleeding |
| **Type 5b** | Definite fatal bleeding |

**Major Bleeding:** BARC Type 3-5

#### 8.3.2 Adverse Event Monitoring

| Category | Reporting |
|----------|-----------|
| All Adverse Events | Documented at each visit |
| Serious Adverse Events (SAEs) | Reported within 24 hours |
| Adverse Events of Special Interest (AESIs) | Expedited reporting |

**AESIs for This Study:**
- All bleeding events (BARC ≥2)
- Gastrointestinal ulceration or perforation
- Allergic/hypersensitivity reactions
- Renal impairment
- Hepatotoxicity

#### 8.3.3 Laboratory Assessments

**Hematology Panel:**
- Complete blood count (CBC) with differential
- Platelet count

**Chemistry Panel:**
- Comprehensive metabolic panel (BMP/CMP)
- Liver function tests (AST, ALT, ALP, total bilirubin)
- Renal function (BUN, creatinine, eGFR)

**Coagulation:**
- PT/INR
- aPTT (if clinically indicated)
